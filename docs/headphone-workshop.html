    Other geometry notes

      Checks update when you apply a layout. Errors identify invalid represented geometry; warnings identify possible contact. Unverified items need additional geometry or part/process data. Shell construction uses a sampled solid; remaining walls and bores need resolution checks. Red parts have placement errors. STL export requires all represented parts to pass containment against the curved shell; tube outer walls are included. A shared acoustic junction model is not implemented. Preview exports do not certify manufacturing or acoustic performance.
