HAMMER CRAFT / ASSISTED TUNING

Human ears. AI assistance.

01

TRAINED TO SUPPORT OUR TUNING PROCESS

Our internal tuning system uses a trained AI-assisted workflow to help analyse frequency-response behaviour, compare targets and evaluate tuning changes.

The AI does not replace listening. It helps us move through iterations faster and identify patterns that would otherwise take significantly longer to assess manually.

02

PERSONALISED DIRECTION

For custom-tuned builds, the system can use a customer's preferred sound direction as another input during development.

Measurements, acoustic engineering and subjective listening remain part of the final decision.

INPUT Target + preference
ANALYSIS AI-assisted comparison
OUTPUT Refined acoustic direction
