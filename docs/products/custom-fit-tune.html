01 OVERVIEW

Your sound. Your geometry.

Full Custom combines the two personalised parts of the Hammer Craft system: a customer-defined acoustic target and a shell designed around reconstructed ear geometry.

The tuning and fit workflows are completed separately, then brought together during the physical design and manufacturing stage.

INTEGRATED DESIGN

Fit and sound cannot be separated.

INTERNAL ARCHITECTURE

The ear defines the available space.

Once the anatomical envelope is established, the acoustic system still has to fit inside it.

Driver placement, acoustic tubes, dampers, electrical components, chamber volumes, connector placement and nozzle geometry all influence the final shell.

DRIVERS

Placement.

Transducer orientation has to work with available internal space and acoustic paths.

TUBING

Routing.

Tube length and diameter can influence the response and must fit within the shell.

NOZZLE

Interface.

Ear geometry and acoustic routing meet at the final nozzle design.

SHELL

Structure.

Wall thickness, access and sealing remain manufacturing constraints even in a custom shell.

QUESTIONS

Full Custom FAQ.

Can I use the tuning tool without logging in?

Yes. Guided Tuning, Advanced FR editing and product matching are public.

When is an account required?

An account is required when you want to save an exact target or associate scan data with a real custom order.

Do I need both a tuning profile and an ear scan?

Yes, for the Full Custom configuration. The tuning profile defines the sound and the ear scan defines the anatomical reference.

What if an existing product already matches my target?

The public tuning tool will recommend it. A custom build is not necessary if an existing product already meets your preference.

Is my reconstructed ear mesh the finished IEM shell?

No. It is used as a reference during shell CAD. The finished shell still needs structural, ergonomic and acoustic design.
