01 OVERVIEW

Same sound. Different geometry.

Custom Fit keeps the Hammer Craft reference response while changing the physical shell from a universal shape to an ear-specific one.

The shell is developed from reconstructed ear geometry, then refined in CAD around acoustic layout, driver placement and fit.

WHY CUSTOM FIT

Built around one pair of ears.

01

Fit.

The shell can be shaped around the customer's own ear geometry rather than relying on a generic universal shell.

02

Stability.

Ear-specific geometry can improve positional stability during movement when the scan and shell are prepared correctly.

03

Internal space.

Custom geometry can also provide a different internal volume and layout envelope for the acoustic system.

CAPTURE WORKFLOW

Many overlapping views.

The customer follows a guided image-capture path around the ear so that the same physical features appear across many neighbouring frames.

The goal is not to take one perfect photograph. The goal is to create enough visual overlap for the reconstruction system to estimate camera positions and recover three-dimensional geometry.

LEFT EAR

Full pass.

The left ear is captured as its own reconstruction dataset.

RIGHT EAR

Separate pass.

The right ear is captured and reconstructed independently.

OVERLAP

Continuity matters.

Sequential images should share enough common detail for reliable feature matching.

03 RECONSTRUCTION
PHOTOGRAMMETRY

Images become geometry.

The reconstruction worker identifies visual features across the image sequence and estimates how the camera moved around the ear.

Sparse geometry is reconstructed first, followed by denser depth information and surface generation.

STEP 01 Features

Distinct image features are detected across the scan sequence.

STEP 02 Matching

Corresponding visual features are matched between neighbouring views.

STEP 03 Camera poses

The reconstruction estimates where each image was captured from.

STEP 04 Dense geometry

Depth and surface information are generated from the registered image set.

MESH PROCESSING

Reconstruction is not the final shell.

RAW MESH

Clean before CAD.

Photogrammetry can generate disconnected geometry, holes, irregular triangles and surfaces that are unsuitable for direct manufacturing.

The reconstruction therefore passes through a mesh-cleanup workflow before it is used as an anatomical reference.

COMPONENTS

Remove noise.

Small disconnected mesh components can be removed from the reconstruction.

REPAIR

Close defects.

Mesh repair can correct winding, normals and recover some open areas.

SOLIDIFICATION

Create usable volume.

Where necessary, the geometry can be converted into a clean closed representation for downstream processing.

DIGITAL FIT EAR GEOMETRY
CAD REFERENCE

The STL is the start, not the product.

Reconstructed ear geometry is used as an anatomical reference for shell development.

The final IEM shell still needs to account for comfort, insertion depth, nozzle placement, wall thickness, driver volume and internal acoustic routing.

CUSTOM SHELL CAD

Anatomy meets acoustic layout.

INTERNAL ARCHITECTURE

Fit cannot be designed alone.

A custom shell must still contain the complete acoustic and electrical system.

Driver placement, tubing, acoustic dampers, connectors, chamber volume and nozzle structures all have to coexist within the available anatomical envelope.

NOZZLE

Position.

Ear geometry influences possible nozzle direction and insertion geometry.

WALLS

Thickness.

Shell walls must balance available internal space against physical strength.

FACEPLATE

Closure.

The final shell needs a repeatable interface for assembly and sealing.

ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

From CAD to resin.

The completed shell geometry can be prepared for resin additive manufacturing.

Orientation, supports, drainage, layer height, resin exposure and post-processing all affect the quality of the final printed shell.

QUESTIONS

Custom Fit FAQ.

Do I need an account to read about Custom Fit?

No. Product information is public.

Do I need an account to scan my ears?

An account is recommended before submitting a real production scan so the resulting geometry can be securely associated with the correct customer and order.

Is the reconstructed STL printed directly?

No. The reconstructed geometry is an anatomical reference. The final IEM shell still requires dedicated CAD development.

Does Custom Fit also change the tuning?

No. Custom Fit uses the Hammer Craft reference tuning. For both custom fit and custom sound, choose Custom Fit + Tune.

What happens if the scan quality is poor?

A reconstruction that does not contain enough usable geometry should be rejected and the customer asked to repeat the scan rather than designing a shell from unreliable anatomy.
