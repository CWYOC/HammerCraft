01 OVERVIEW

Keep the fit. Change the sound.

Custom Tune uses the Hammer Craft universal-fit physical platform, but replaces the fixed reference response with a customer-defined target.

The target can be generated through simple listening-preference questions or built manually using the public Advanced FR editor.

02 SOUND
CUSTOM TARGET

Your target becomes the reference.

The customer's curve is treated as the target response for the build.

Rather than describing the sound only with labels such as warm, neutral or bright, Hammer Craft stores the actual frequency-response target used during development and verification.

SUB-BASS 20–80 Hz

Controls low-frequency extension and rumble.

MID-BASS 80–200 Hz

Controls punch and perceived bass weight.

WARMTH 200–600 Hz

Influences lower-mid body and fullness.

VOCALS 1–3 kHz

Influences vocal position and presence.

UPPER MIDS 2.5–5 kHz

Controls clarity, presence and energy.

TREBLE 5–10 kHz

Influences brightness and detail.

AIR 10 kHz+

Influences openness and upper extension.

TUNING WORKFLOW

Two ways to define your target.

GUIDED TUNING

Describe the sound, not the graph.

Guided Tuning is designed for customers who know what they like but do not want to work directly with frequency-response data.

Questions about bass quantity, bass character, warmth, vocal position, treble preference, sensitivity and music taste are converted into a structured target.

ADVANCED FR

Direct control.

Experienced users can manipulate frequency control points directly.

RESOLUTION

PHYSICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Turning the curve into hardware.

SYSTEM APPROACH

The target is not an EQ preset.

Custom Tune is intended to be implemented in the physical IEM rather than simply saved as digital EQ.

The final response depends on the interaction between drivers, electrical filters, acoustic damping, tubing, chambers, venting and nozzle geometry.

DRIVER BALANCE

Level.

Driver output can be adjusted electrically and acoustically to change the contribution of each frequency region.

FILTERING

Crossover.

Electrical and acoustic roll-offs are developed together rather than independently.

ACOUSTIC PATH

Tube + damper.

Sound-tube dimensions and acoustic resistance can alter resonance, bandwidth and level.

VENTING

Pressure.

Front and rear vent behaviour can influence low-frequency response and driver loading.

QUESTIONS

Custom Tune FAQ.

Do I need an account to use the tuning tool?

No. The tuning tool, Guided Tuning, Advanced FR editor and product matching can all be used publicly.

When do I need to log in?

Login is only required when you want to save an exact custom target to your account or use it in a custom order.

What happens if an existing product already matches me?

The tuning tool will show that product as a recommendation. You can open its product page and choose it instead of paying for a custom build.

Is the 0.1 dB editor the same as digital EQ?

No. The editor defines the requested acoustic target. The intended Custom Tune workflow then implements that target through the physical IEM.

Do I need to scan my ears?

No. Custom Tune uses a universal shell. Ear scanning is only required for custom-fit products.
