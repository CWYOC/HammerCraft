01 OVERVIEW

The fixed point in a custom system.

Standard is the reference configuration used to define the Hammer Craft baseline. It gives us a repeatable physical and acoustic platform before custom tuning or ear-specific shell geometry is introduced.

For the customer, that means a straightforward route into the Hammer Craft system without creating a tuning profile or completing a digital ear scan.

HC REFERENCE

Designed as a complete response.

Standard is not intended to be defined by a single exaggerated frequency region.

Bass extension, midrange presence and treble energy are developed together as one target, then checked through measurement.

SUB-BASS Extended

Low-frequency reach without relying only on elevated mid-bass.

MID-BASS Controlled

Impact and body without excessive lower-mid masking.

MIDRANGE Balanced

Vocal and instrument presence without pushing the entire midrange forward.

TREBLE Controlled

Detail and extension balanced against long-term listening comfort.

ACOUSTIC SYSTEM

More than a driver list.

COMPLETE SYSTEM

The response is created by interaction.

Driver behaviour alone does not determine the final response of an IEM.

Electrical filtering, acoustic damping, sound-tube geometry, chamber volume, venting, nozzle interaction and physical layout all influence what reaches the ear.

CROSSOVER

Electrical.

Components can control the operating range and relative contribution of individual transducers.

DAMPING

Acoustic.

Acoustic resistance can help control resonances, bandwidth and output.

TUBING

Geometry.

Tube diameter and length can alter resonant behaviour before sound reaches the nozzle.

VENTING

Loading.

Vent geometry affects pressure behaviour and can materially alter low-frequency response.

QUESTIONS

Standard FAQ.

Is Standard custom tuned?

No. Standard uses the fixed Hammer Craft reference response.

Do I need an account to view or compare it?

No. Product information and the public tuning-matching tool are available without login.

Do I need to scan my ears?

No. Standard uses a universal-fit shell.

What if I like Standard but want a custom shell?

Choose Custom Fit, which keeps the reference tuning while using ear-specific geometry.

What if I want to change the response?

Use the public tuning tool first. If no existing product is close enough, Custom Tune or Full Custom can use your saved target.

NOT SURE?

Compare it with your target.

Use the public Hammer Craft tuning tool to see how closely Standard matches the response you choose.
