01 / OUR STORY

Small team.
Big ideas
in sound.

Hammer Craft is a small independent startup based in Bristol, UK.

We started with a simple idea: every ear is different, every listener is different, so why should everyone have to listen to exactly the same tuning?

Being a young company allows us to stay hands-on with what we build. From acoustic development and AI-assisted tuning to 3D-printed shells and final assembly, our goal is to treat each IEM as something we craft rather than simply manufacture.

We combine acoustic engineering, digital manufacturing and software-driven tuning to explore a more personal approach to headphone design.

03 / AI-ASSISTED TUNING

Your sound.
Refined by intelligence.

Behind our custom tuning process is a purpose-trained AI system developed to assist us with headphone and IEM acoustic tuning.

The system helps us analyse listening preferences, target frequency responses and acoustic measurements, giving us another tool for exploring how an IEM can be adjusted toward the sound you want.

It can help us compare measurements, identify differences between a current response and a desired target, and assist with evaluating possible tuning changes.

But AI isn't the final decision-maker. Measurements, acoustic engineering and human listening remain part of the process. The system works alongside us rather than replacing our judgement.
