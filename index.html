03 / AI-ASSISTED TUNING

Your sound.
Refined by intelligence.

Hammer Craft uses a purpose-trained AI system designed specifically to assist our headphone and IEM tuning workflow.

The system helps us analyse listening preferences, acoustic measurements and target frequency responses, allowing us to explore tuning options more efficiently and consistently.

Rather than relying only on generic presets, we can compare your preferred sound signature against measurements from the IEM and use the system to assist with refinement.

AI does not replace our engineering judgement. It works alongside our acoustic design process, measurements and listening tests to help us make more informed tuning decisions.
