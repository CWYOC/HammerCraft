Instead of expecting every listener to adapt to one product, we're developing products that can adapt to the listener.

004 / HAMMER CRAFT AI

Your taste. Our tuning system.

Our trained AI helps us turn your listening preferences into useful guidance for tuning your headphones.

Rather than simply asking you to choose between a few presets, our system is being developed to understand how you describe sound and translate those preferences into useful tuning targets.

AI assists the tuning process rather than replacing acoustic engineering. Measurements, driver behaviour, crossover design, acoustic chambers and listening tests remain part of every design.

005 / DIGITAL FIT

Your ears are part of the design.

We're developing a guided digital ear-scanning system for Hammer Craft custom-fit IEMs.

On supported devices, the system captures your left and right ear geometry and sends the scans to Hammer Craft for processing.
